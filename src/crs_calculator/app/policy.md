### About this calculator

This is an **unofficial** educational tool. It reproduces the points tables
published by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). Your official
score is the one calculated in your Express Entry profile. Points do not
guarantee an invitation to apply.

### Rule sets

| Rule set | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Current rules (since March 25, 2025)** | No CRS points for job offers. Canadian work experience is scored in the core factors (max 80, or 70 with a spouse). |
| **Labour Market Integration factor (from November 3, 2026)** | Canadian work experience is scored as work-length points + high-wage occupation points (20 / 40 / 70), **or** a qualifying high-wage job offer (50 / 70 / 100), whichever is higher. Max 150 (140 with a spouse). No LMIA needed. Skill transferability still uses work-length only. |

The app selects the rule set in force on today's date by default; you can switch at any time.

### Maximum points

| Section | With spouse | Without spouse |
|---|---|---|
| A. Core / human capital | 460 | 500 |
| B. Spouse or common-law partner | 40 | n/a |
| C. Skill transferability | 100 | 100 |
| D. Additional points | 600 | 600 |

### Modelling assumptions

* You are scored "without a spouse" when your spouse/partner is not coming to
  Canada or is a Canadian citizen or permanent resident.
* CLB/NCLC 10 or higher scores the same as CLB 10. Results below CLB 4 are shown as "3".
* **French bonus:** 50 points when French is NCLC 7+ on all four abilities **and**
  English is CLB 5+ on all four; 25 points when French is NCLC 7+ and English
  does not meet CLB 5 on all four (or there is no English test).
* Skill transferability language thresholds use the **first** official language only.
* Canadian post-secondary bonus points are ignored if your highest education is secondary or less.
* High-wage occupation points require at least one year of Canadian work experience.
* Simulations set all four abilities of a language to the same level.
